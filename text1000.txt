The Internet of Things (IoT) refers to the network of physical devices, vehicles, home appliances, and other items embedded with sensors, software, and connectivity. These devices collect and exchange data, enabling them to interact and be controlled remotely. IoT has applications in various fields, including smart homes, healthcare, agriculture, and industrial automation. For example, in smart homes, IoT devices can control lighting, temperature, and security systems. In healthcare, IoT can monitor patients' health in real-time. Despite its benefits, IoT also raises concerns about data privacy and security. As the number of connected devices grows, addressing these challenges will be crucial.
